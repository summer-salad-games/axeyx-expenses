# Common password blocklist

The first 3,000 distinct UTF-8 entries of at least 12 characters from SecLists xato-net-10-million-passwords-1000000.txt, preserving source rank for selection and sorting for storage. Used only for exact, case-sensitive rejection of newly chosen/provisioned passwords; passwords are never modified.

Source: https://github.com/danielmiessler/SecLists/blob/180c2d55afa7aab8b18b7552f82dd0c92cc4ede5/Passwords/Common-Credentials/xato-net-10-million-passwords-1000000.txt
